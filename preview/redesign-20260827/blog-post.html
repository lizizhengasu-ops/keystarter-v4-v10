Windows & Microsoft AI News: ALLAM in Foundry

Aug 27, 2026 KeyStarter Editorial Team 8 min read
On this page 01. ALLAM enters Microsoft Foundry 02. AI gateways become attack targets 03. Azure SRE Agent free trial 04. WebView2 moves to two-week releases 05. Teams Asia-Pacific outage restored

Microsoft deepened its Saudi AI partnership by bringing HUMAIN ALLAM into Foundry and M365 Copilot, while new research shows AI gateways are becoming prime attack targets. Windows and Microsoft 365 teams should treat AI infrastructure as a control plane and plan for WebView2's faster release cadence.

01. ALLAM enters Microsoft Foundry

Microsoft and HUMAIN announced a long-term strategic collaboration under which ALLAM will be available in Microsoft Foundry and M365 Copilot. The deal pairs Saudi national AI development with Microsoft's enterprise stack: Arabic-language professional agents, joint engineering through FDE, and a preview at LEAP.

For enterprises, the practical takeaway is simple: Arabic-language Copilot agents are becoming an officially supported scenario, not an experimental add-on.

02. AI gateways become attack targets

Microsoft security research published on August 26 describes real campaigns against AI infrastructure, including credential theft through LiteLLM gateways, persistence via RAGFlow, and compute monetization through Kestra. AI gateways are high-value control planes because they sit between users, models and data.

03. Azure SRE Agent free trial

Microsoft announced a no-always-on-cost trial for Azure SRE Agent, with usage-based pricing, VNet integration GA, Live Reports public preview, and internal evidence of roughly 50% autonomous resolution across 1.5 million events. One surveyed customer cut investigation time by about 80%.

04. WebView2 moves to two-week releases

WebView2 is shifting to a two-week release cadence. Build 152 is the last four-week release in the week of August 27; build 153 ships in the week of September 10. Runtime and SDK release on the same day, Evergreen clients need no action, and Fixed Version deployments need more frequent validation.

05. Teams Asia-Pacific outage restored

A maintenance activity in Microsoft 365 affected Teams in Asia-Pacific, including Japan. Microsoft paused maintenance and restored standard availability around 10:47 JST. The incident is a reminder to review your org's maintenance notification subscriptions.
