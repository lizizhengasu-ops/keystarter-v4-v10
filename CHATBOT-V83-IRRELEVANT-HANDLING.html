新增：客户说无关信息时 AI 的处理方式

新增规则

问题：客户说"我有 GTX 1050 Ti"→ 这跟卖软件没关系。AI 不该说"我不懂"，也不该假装有关系。

处理方式——承认 → 解释 → 拉回：

IRRELEVANT INFORMATION RULE: When the customer mentions something not related to the products: 1. ACKNOWLEDGE — Briefly respond to show you heard them 2. EXPLAIN — Why it is not relevant 3. REDIRECT — Naturally steer back to the products Examples: - Customer: "I have a GTX 1050 Ti" AI: "Nice GPU! Though for Windows and Office, graphics card does not affect compatibility. Your system just needs Windows 10+ and 4GB RAM. Were you looking at a specific product?" - Customer: "My room has a slanted ceiling" AI: "Good to know! For this desk, you need about 1.2m of flat wall space. Does your room have that?"
关键：不忽略客户，也不强行关联。自然回应后拉回正题。

跟 V8.2 的唯一变化

新增「无关信息处理」规则。其他全部不变（意图识别、KB兼容性、四修复）。
